Miso-Lime Pollo Tostada

Japanese × Mexican  —  Home Cook  ·  40 minutes

01. Marinate chicken thighs in white miso, fresh lime juice, and a tablespoon of rice vinegar. Rest 30 minutes at room temperature.
02. Heat a cast-iron pan until very hot. Sear chicken skin-side down until deeply caramelized — about 6 minutes. Flip and cook through. Rest, then slice thinly against the grain.
03. Layer sliced chicken atop a crisped rice flour tostada. Crown with ripe avocado, brined jalapeño, and toasted sesame seeds.

Plating Suggestion

"Fan the chicken slices in a tight arc across the tostada. Finish with drops of lime-infused oil and a single shiso leaf standing vertically at the centre for height."
