Miso-Lime Pollo Tostada

Japanese × Mexican  —  Home Cook  ·  40 min

Plating Suggestion

"Fan the chicken slices in a tight arc across the tostada. Finish with drops of lime-infused oil and a single shiso leaf standing vertically at the centre for height and elegance."

01

Marinate chicken thighs in white miso, fresh lime juice, and a tablespoon of rice vinegar. Rest for 30 minutes at room temperature to allow the flavors to penetrate.

02

Heat a cast-iron pan over high heat until smoking. Sear chicken skin-side down until deeply caramelized — approximately 6 minutes. Flip and cook through. Rest 5 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain.

03

Layer sliced chicken atop a crisped rice flour tostada. Crown with ripe avocado, brined jalapeño, a scatter of toasted sesame seeds, and a squeeze of fresh lime to finish.
